# Strict luciferase pilot results

Run date: 2026-09-08

Four miRNA–gene pairs met the full-text-verified WT/mutant 3′UTR criteria:
miR-16-5p–BCL2, miR-17-5p–BMPR2, miR-25-3p–PTEN, and
miR-124-3p–RAB27A. All four were observable in the frozen multi-context rank
table and all four fell in the top APA-exposure decile. Their mean APA-exposure
percentile was 0.9673 and median was 0.9733.

Adding miR-155-5p–ETS1 in the separately labelled construct-corroborated
sensitivity scope produced five observable pairs; all five fell in the top
decile (mean 0.9668, median 0.9649).

These are descriptive construct-audit results, not a performance estimate.
The pairs were reviewed after the broad reporter benchmark had been examined,
and the eligible sample is only four or five pairs. No p-value, bootstrap
interval, or claim of generalization is therefore reported. The full audit,
including excluded and unresolved candidates, is retained in
`curated/strict_luciferase_audit.tsv`.
